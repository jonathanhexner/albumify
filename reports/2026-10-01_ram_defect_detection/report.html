Can RAM++ detect defects? Occlusion & blur, measured

RAM++ is a content tagger. This tests, against labeled data, whether it can double as a defect detector — and compares it to the purpose-built models already in the pipeline. AUC bars are scaled 0.50 (chance) → 1.00 (perfect).

Conclusion

RAM++ is the wrong tool for both defects — confirmed on labeled data, not asserted. Occlusion: even its best-correlated tag of 4,585 (AUC 0.90) trails the dedicated probe (0.98); hand/lens flare are near chance (0.38). Blur: no blur/focus/sharp tag exists and none ever fires, so there is no blur score to produce. Keep occlusion on the trained probe and blur/quality on IQA/noise-aware sharpness; use RAM++ only for the content axis.
