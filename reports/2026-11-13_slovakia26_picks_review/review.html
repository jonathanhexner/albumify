How to review (click to hide)
  • One scene at a time. Green border = the app picked it. The others are what it skipped, with the reason.
  • For each pick: Keep or Drop, and on Drop tick why. For a skipped photo you would want in the album: Should pick.
  • Scene OK keeps every undecided pick and moves on. Use it whenever the scene looks right.
  • Click a photo for a large view. Keys there: K keep, D drop, M should pick, ←/→ move, Esc close. Outside it: ←/→ change scene.
  • Everything saves in this browser as you go. When done, Export review and save picks_review.json into this folder.
