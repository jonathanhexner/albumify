Verdict: the frozen-CLIP probe beats the fine-tuned CNN by ~13 scene PR-AUC points (0.899 vs 0.768) with 5× fewer false positives at the gate. Training loss fell to ~0.01 (the CNN memorized the training set) while OOF stayed far behind — 80 positives is simply not enough data to fine-tune a CNN, even with heavy augmentation, whereas CLIP's 400M-image pretraining already encodes the needed invariances and the LR head only has ~1k parameters to fit. Augmentation helped neither model (CLIP: null; ResNet: still overfits through it). Consistent with spec-096's frozen-ResNet50 result (0.66).
