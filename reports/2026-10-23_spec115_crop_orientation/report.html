spec-115 T2.1 — crop orientation gate (slovakia26)

Goal. Before the worker stops sending face crops, prove the PC can cut the same faces from its own 768 px copies using the face boxes in results/bundle.json, including photos with an EXIF rotation.

Method. 20 faces with a worker crop, 5 per EXIF orientation (1, 6, 3, 8). PC crop = the box cut from the photo read like the detector reads it (ImageOps.exif_transpose). ArcFace cosine (buffalo_l) between the PC crop and the worker crop; bar set before the run: ≥ 0.3 on ≥ 19 of 20. Negative control: the rotated photos cut without the transpose must not match.

Result. 18/20 above the bar (needed 19): FAIL by the pre-set number. Control rejected 14/15 (the one miss is an 11×13 px face). Inspection of the 3 low rows: all PC crops show the right face. Misses: face 23 is a tilted head (the worker aligns faces on 5 landmarks, a box crop does not); face 3 and every other pair show red and blue swapped in the worker's crops (mean R−B has the opposite sign in all 5 pairs checked), which lowers every score: SIGHTING-141.

Full image set: ~/.sim_bench/jobs/slovakia26/ (inputs, worker crops). Sample crops are in img/ beside this file, not in git.
