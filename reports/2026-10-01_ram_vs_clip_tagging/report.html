RAM++ vs fixed-method CLIP — content tagging on 30 trip photos

RAM++ = open-vocabulary tagger (swin_l/384, ~4.5k tags, ~2.8 GB). CLIP = ViT-B/32 laion2b read with the fixed method (per-category softmax over the 20 scene prompts + people gating) — a closed 20-class vocabulary. The question isn't quality (CLIP can't judge that) — it's whether RAM++'s larger open vocabulary earns a new model on the pipeline's memory floor.
