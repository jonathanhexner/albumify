Defect scoring #4: Rotation (wrong orientation)

Goal. The pipeline's only orientation handling is EXIF-based (ImageOps.exif_transpose in rule_based.py; landmark-based orientation exists only for face crops). Photos with wrong or stripped EXIF (screenshots, WhatsApp exports, scanned prints) rotate undetected. Can any scorer detect content rotation?

Method

  • Dataset: generated — 122 real album photos (D:\Budapest2025_Google), EXIF-normalized, downscaled to 1024 px, then pixel-rotated to 0/90/180/270° and re-encoded (strips EXIF) = 488 variants. Variants kept in data/variants/.
  • Ours: pipeline overall rule-based score — expected rotation-invariant (histograms and gradient statistics barely change under 90° rotations).
  • Classical: sky prior — top third of a correctly oriented photo should be brighter than the bottom third.
  • SOTA: CLIP ViT-B/32 zero-shot — upright score = sim("right side up") − max(sim("rotated sideways"), sim("upside down")).
  • Metrics: 4-way accuracy = the upright variant gets the highest score among a photo's 4 rotations (chance 25%); AUC = upright vs rotated separability (chance 0.5).
  • Reproduce: .venv/Scripts/python scripts/experiment_defectscore_rotation.py. Raw scores: data/scores.csv.

Our score is exactly chance: mean 0.81056 upright vs 0.81057 rotated — the pipeline is blind to orientation by construction, as predicted. The sky prior gets half of outdoor shots but fails indoors and on 90° rotations. CLIP zero-shot — with three text prompts and no training — reaches 82%; its misses are mostly close-ups and symmetric scenes with no gravity cue.

Verdict

The pipeline cannot detect a rotated photo (AUC 0.500) — acceptable while EXIF is trustworthy, broken for EXIF-stripped sources. CLIP zero-shot is a cheap viable detector (82% 4-way, no training, model already in the stack from spec-097); a face-orientation cross-check could raise it further on people shots. If rotation detection is wanted, propose a spec: flag when CLIP upright-score for the current orientation is beaten by a rotated variant, on EXIF-less images only. Dedicated orientation CNNs (~98% in literature) are the upgrade path if CLIP proves insufficient.
