Engineering review · 2026-11-13

Albumify: path to production

Live photo-album curator (upload a trip, get the best photos). Repo github.com/jonathanhexner/albumify · roadmap ROADMAP.md · prepared for the head of software.

Bottom line
  • The engine runs end to end from the app. On this PC with local storage, and on the cloud GPU (Modal L4), proven 2026-10-23. On the new cloud database (Neon) + file store (R2): a full 715-photo run started from the app completes and shows in Albums, Results and People. Not yet proven: the GPU writing to Neon (needs a deploy and owner OK for spend), and acceptable page speed on Neon (Results takes 28-54 s vs 2.9 s locally).
  • Quality is close to the bar: on a 715-photo trip the owner kept 10/10 of the top 10 picks and 28/30 of the top 30 (bar: ≥ 90%). The full list of 302 picks is weaker (73%), so the product will show Best 10 / Best 30 / All.
  • Not shippable yet. Open items before merging: Neon page speed (SIGHTING-152), a Results gallery crash (SIGHTING-151), the GPU-on-Neon proof, and spec-116's open sighting. Then Gate 1 needs its formal verdict. The public web front end and hardening (Stage D) have not started.
  • Main risk is scope creep into quality tuning. Seven improvement ideas are parked in spec-118, each measurable against the saved owner review.
