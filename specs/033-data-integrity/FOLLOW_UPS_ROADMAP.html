spec-033 · Follow-up Roadmap

Executive summary of work surfaced by the spec-033 code review · 2026-05-15

Outcome to head-of-engineering. The spec-033 refactor is mergeable but not "done." The code review surfaced 8 follow-up items, of which 5 deserve proper PRDs (full spec dirs), 2 are bug-shaped sightings, and 1 is trivial housekeeping. The highest-leverage item is FR-033-1: an E2E acceptance test — its absence is the reason SIGHTING-061 went undetected and is the single most useful piece of work to schedule next.

FR-033-1 E2E acceptance test for Albumify face-clustering pipeline Critical PRD: specs/035 The architecture-test suite is all static + unit; zero tests exercise the real pipeline on real data. This is what would have caught SIGHTING-061.
FR-033-2 Config-to-producer graph contract Major PRD: specs/036 A new class of contract the refactor exposed the need for but didn't build: "every gate the bridge honors must have an upstream step producing its field."
