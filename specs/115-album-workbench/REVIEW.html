Part 1 — How it works

See the spec's plan and class diagram and docs/architecture/data_flow.html ("spec-115"). In short: photos are resized once into a content-addressed store; a job is a manifest of keys; the runner (local child or Modal L4) returns results only; the server renders displays and crops itself; one Modal job runs at a time so the feature cache on its Volume has one writer.
