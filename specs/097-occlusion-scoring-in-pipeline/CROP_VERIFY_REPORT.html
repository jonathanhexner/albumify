0 · The idea being tested (your proposal)

Problem:  the LoG blur-box proposer finds ALL flat-but-noisy regions —
          real fingers (2/2 ✅) but also sky/haze (45 false boxes on 122 Budapest images).

Your idea: don't fight the proposer — VERIFY each candidate with CLIP:

   image ──► LoG proposer ──► candidate box ──► CROP it ──► ask CLIP: occluder or scene?
             (cheap, high recall)                            (the crop IS the defect —
                                                             no subject to distract CLIP)

Test set: the 47 REAL candidate crops from Budapest (2 real fingers + 45 false alarms).
Success = the 2 fingers rank at the TOP of 47 when sorted by "occluder-ness".

3 · Experiment 2 — learned probe on crops (and why it's INVALID)

Design (zero manual labels): train a logistic head on CLIP embeddings of synthetic occluder crops (positives, recovered by diffing synthetic composites against their base images) vs self-mined false-alarm crops (negatives — whatever the proposer wrongly boxes on 554 Austria/Germany clean images). Test on the 47 real Budapest crops.

Post-mortem — the experiment was flawed, and here is the evidence. I extracted positive "crops" by bounding every pixel the compositor changed. But the occluder's soft alpha edge faintly touches huge areas, so the diff-box often spanned the whole photo:
So experiment 2 does NOT settle whether synthetic training transfers. It settles that my crop extraction was wrong. The question "is it just a matter of training data?" (your words) remains open — and is exactly what the two follow-ups below test properly.

4 · What I propose (in order)

1Real-crop probe (~30 min, existing data): train on the LoG-box crops of the 49 non-Budapest REAL positives vs the mined false-alarm crops; test unchanged on the 47 Budapest crops. Every approach that ever worked on this problem trained on real data — this is that approach, at crop level.
2Redo synthetic properly: tight occluder masks (threshold the diff at the blob CORE, largest component only — or regenerate the exact ellipse from the deterministic compositor), so positives are actual blob crops. Only worth it if #1 shows the concept works but starves for data.

Now genuinely valuable: ~30–50 shots would roughly double the real positive pool for BOTH the whole-image detector and the crop verifier.
