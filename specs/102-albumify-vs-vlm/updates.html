2026-10-09 — Testing method clarified (recorded by the owner)

  • Decision: PhotoTriage / CUFED benchmarks are NOT the quality gate for Albumify. PhotoTriage is best-of-burst on near-duplicate series, not a real album; CUFED is importance voting. Neither says "would these 20 picks make a good trip album." The gate is real trips (Slovakia26, Budapest, Austria, Germany) judged against the owner's own keeper list.
  • Follow-up completed 2026-10-09: roadmap Stage A now uses real trips judged against the owner's keeper list; PhotoTriage/CUFED removed from the quality gate in line with this recorded decision.
  • Note: this decision was not written down earlier; the written trail (finalization plan, agentic_layer_design) proposed PhotoTriage/CUFED. Recorded here so it is not re-proposed.

2026-10-01 — Slovakia26 real-roll analysis (branch-out to specs 107–109)

  • What: ran the faces pipeline (CPU, 768px, K=20) on a 75-image subset of the real 715-photo Slovakia26 trip with screenshots deliberately left in.
  • Finding: 6 of 20 picks were junk (5 screenshots + 1 photographed poster). A Gmail screenshot scored 0.703, the top score in the album. Junk mean composite 0.54 vs 0.41 for real photos: quality scoring rewards screenshots, so they must be removed before curation, not filtered by score.
  • Also: the 14 real picks were sound trip moments across 8 scene clusters. Runtime 2.2 s/img with the face stack at 75% of it.
  • Branched out: spec-107 (input triage), spec-108 (semantic layer), spec-109 (GPU). The quality verdict itself was deferred until triage is on and a keeper list exists.
  • Verified by: reports/2026-10-01_slovakia_analysis/ (report.html with inline samples, summary.md, results.json). Repro: scripts/experiment_slovakia_analysis.py.

2026-07-17/18 — Both arms + 3 trips (Budapest, Austria, Germany)

  • What: Albumify arm (real pipeline on 768px inputs, exactly K=20) vs VLM arm (Claude Opus, raw images only, map-shortlist then reduce), blind A/B viewer, metrics, structured annotation (EXP-3).
  • Finding: pick overlap shrinks with trip size: Budapest 6/20, Austria 3/20, Germany 0/20. Full people-aware pipeline OOMed above ~120 images (SIGHTING-117), so the big trips ran the minimal pipeline without the person penalty.
