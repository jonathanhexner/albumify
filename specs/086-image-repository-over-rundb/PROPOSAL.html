spec-086 — ImageRepository over run_db

Decision one-pager for approval · 2026-06-25 · Slice 1 of the "SQL tables + repository" migration

The decision, in 4 lines

  • Add 1 table: image_selection (is_selected, reason) to the existing normalized run_db.
  • Add 1 repository: ImageRepository.get_images_for_person() — a real SQL JOIN, not JSON stitching.
  • Reuse 3 existing tables: images, faces, face_scores (already built, already have the scores & bboxes).
  • Why now: fixes the People & Faces blank-filter / no-face-box bugs the right way, and proves the pattern for the wider migration.
