The pipeline

Four passes, the way an editor works a light table.

Every frame runs a real computer-vision pipeline — not a vibe check. Here's what each pass decides.

PASS 01

Score the frame

Noise-aware sharpness, exposure, aesthetics and occlusion — so a blurred or blocked shot never makes the cut.

AVA · IQA · CLIP occlusion · tilt / auto-straighten
PASS 02

Collapse the dupes

Near-identical frames of the same moment are grouped; only the strongest survives the group.

DINOv2 scene embeddings · time + GPS fusion
PASS 03

Find the people

Faces are detected, embedded and clustered — so the album knows who's in it, and no one gets left out.

InsightFace · kNN graph · conservative merge
PASS 04

Tell the story

Keepers are ordered for coverage and chronology — a sequence that reads like a trip, not a pile.

coverage-first selection · story ordering
